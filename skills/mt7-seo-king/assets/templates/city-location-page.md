# City / location page brief - with a legitimacy gate

`[G]` Doorway abuse explicitly includes "pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" and "substantially similar pages" (Spam policies, 2026-08-28). `[REPORTED]` Mueller on 1,300 keyword+city pages: "That sounds like doorway pages". `[INFERENCE]` Google never requires an office in the city; a page for a city where you have real clients is legitimate if it shows that work. A page that only swaps the city name is a doorway.

## LEGITIMACY GATE - the page is blocked unless EVERY box is ticked
- [ ] You have **real local proof**: at least 2 clients, projects or staff in this city that you may name (permission on file), OR a staffed office that receives customers there.
- [ ] The page will contain **city-specific substance that cannot be reused for another city**: named local clients and what was built, how you meet people there, local people, local address/service-area facts, local photos.
- [ ] **No other city page shares its copy.** Diff it; shared paragraphs beyond nav/footer fail the gate.
- [ ] The page sits in a **browseable hierarchy** (linked from nav/footer or a "Locations" hub), not only from sitemaps `[G]`.
- [ ] You are **not** planning more than a handful of these. A list of 20+ cities with the same template is the policy's own example.

If any box is unticked: do not build the page. Put the proof on the industry page or the case study instead, and revisit when a real client exists in that city.

## Fill-in
- City (as locals write it): ...  Real connection (office / clients / registration / staff): ...
- Primary query: "<service> in <city>" / "<service> company <city>"  Verified SERP format: ...
- URL: /<city-slug> or /locations/<city-slug>  Title: ...  Meta description: ...  h1: ...

## Skeleton
1. **Answer-first:** what you do in this city and for whom; how engagements actually run here (in person / remote + visits).
2. **Local clients and work** (the proof): each with what was built, a result, a screenshot, a quote - with permission.
3. **How to meet you here:** office address if real and staffed (NAP identical to GBP `[DATA]` [Whitespark, 2025-11](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)), or service area + how visits work. No virtual office `[G]`.
4. **People here:** named staff/founder with roles.
5. **Services offered in this city** - linked to the service pages (not re-described).
6. **Local context that is true:** industries you serve here, local platforms/integrations, local pricing in local currency.
7. **Reviews:** real review text quoted, without self-serving rating schema `[G]`.
8. **CTA** with the local contact route.

## GBP interplay `[G]`
- One Business Profile per real, staffed location only. A city without a staffed location gets a page (organic) but no profile.
- Service-area businesses: areas within ~2 h of the base; cities farther away are organic-only.

## Do not
- Create "<service> in <city>" for every nearby city `[G]` `[WARNED]` (the Aug 2026 spam update's case studies were scaled, templated sites that lost 14K-200K+ queries, [GSQi, 2026-08-31](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/)).
- Put the city or service in the GBP name `[G]`.
- Stuff the city name into every heading; write it where a human would.
