# Industry page brief ("<service> for <industry>")

A page per industry you have really served, anchored on distinct case evidence. `[PROVEN]` "[service] for [industry]" is a winnable bottom-of-funnel pattern for low-authority sites; `[G]` the same page with industry names swapped is scaled/doorway content.

## Gate
- [ ] At least one delivered project in this industry with a measurable outcome you may publish.
- [ ] The page will contain industry-specific substance (regulations, workflows, integrations, metrics) that no other industry page shares.
- [ ] The SERP for "<service> for <industry>" shows vendor pages (not only directories/listicles).

## Fill-in
- Industry (buyer's term): ...  Service(s): ...
- Primary query: ...  Related: "<industry> <software/website/app> cost", "<industry> <tool> integration", "<problem> in <industry>"
- URL: /industries/<slug>  Title: ...  Meta description: ...  h1: ...

## Skeleton
1. **Answer-first:** what you build for this industry and the outcome it produces, in 3 sentences.
2. **The industry's specific problems** you have actually solved (3-5, each one sentence of problem + one of what you did).
3. **Anchor case study** (required): client (with permission), scope, difficulty, measured result, screenshot, quote. Link to the full case.
4. **What is different about building for this industry:** integrations, compliance, data, seasonality - concrete, first-hand `[G]` original information.
5. **Scope and pricing range** for typical engagements, in text.
6. **Buyer questions** in prose.
7. **Related services** linked with descriptive anchors; **CTA**.

## Rule for proof placement
Evidence from a client in a city where you have no other presence belongs on the **industry** page, not a city page (e.g. a resort client in a hill town -> "software for resorts and homestays", not "<hill town> web agency"). `[INFERENCE]` from the doorway policy.

## Technical
Self-canonical, sitemap, nav/footer link, internal links from the case study and the service page. Service or Organization schema only with real fields. Run `scripts/onpage-check.mjs`.

## Do not
Generate industry pages from a list of industries you have not served; reuse paragraphs across industry pages; invent "typical results".
