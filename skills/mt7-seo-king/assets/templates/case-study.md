# Case study brief

Case studies are the proof layer every other page links to and the only honest source of "results" claims. `[CLAIM]` Portfolio/case pages bring leads (vendor-reported); `[DATA]` Zyppy: "proprietary assets" r = 0.36 with winning. `[G]` Original reporting and first-hand expertise are what raters are told to reward.

## Gate
- [ ] Client permission to publish name, screenshots and numbers (written, on file). If not, anonymise honestly ("a 40-room resort in Karnataka") and say so.
- [ ] Every number is measured, dated, and reproducible (Lighthouse report, analytics export, booking count) - never invented or rounded up.
- [ ] A real difficulty exists to describe. A case with no difficulty is a portfolio entry, not a case study.

## Fill-in
- Client: ...  Industry: ...  City: ...  Service(s): ...  Dates: ...
- Primary query this case supports (usually the service or industry page's query, plus "<stack> <thing> example"): ...
- URL: /work/<slug>  Title: "<Client>: <outcome in 5 words> | <Brand>"  Meta description: ...  h1: ...

## Skeleton
1. **Summary box:** client, what was built, stack, timeline, the headline measured result with its date and method.
2. **Context:** who the client is, what they had before, why it mattered.
3. **Difficulty:** the hard part, technically or commercially - specific.
4. **What we did:** decisions and trade-offs, in first person; named people who did the work.
5. **Result:** measured, dated, with the evidence embedded (screenshot of the report, chart from real data). Performance scores are UX/conversion proof, not an SEO claim `[DATA]` (speed ~ zero correlation with rankings).
6. **Client quote** (real, attributed, with permission).
7. **What we would do differently** (credibility).
8. **Links:** to the service page and industry page with descriptive anchors `[G]`; CTA.

## Technical
- Article or CreativeWork JSON-LD with a real `author` (`assets/schema/article.md`); BreadcrumbList.
- Preferred image: a real 16:9 screenshot/photo (`[G]` no text cards as the preferred image).
- Canonical, sitemap, linked from /work, the service page and the industry page.

## Do not
- Publish client booking/revenue figures the client did not approve; headline a client's sensitive numbers.
- Use stock imagery or AI "team" photos `[G]`.
- Cite performance scores below 90 as proof of anything (a sensible rule everywhere) `[INFERENCE]`.
