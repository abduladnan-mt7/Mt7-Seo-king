# "How to choose a <provider type> in <city/country>" guide brief

The honest substitute for a self-ranking "best X in <city>" listicle. `[DATA]` "best X" SERPs are owned by directories and listicles (68% directories for "best attorney": [BrightLocal, 2024-10](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/business-listings-visibility-study/)), so a business page rarely wins them; `[WARNED]` publishing your own ranked list is risky. A buyer's guide targets the adjacent query ("how to choose", "what to look for", "questions to ask", "<provider> cost in <city>") that a business site can legitimately win and that AI engines quote.

## Gate
- [ ] You can write the guide so that a reader could use it to hire a competitor.
- [ ] Every criterion is grounded in real engagements (what went wrong, what to check) - first-hand `[G]`.
- [ ] The city/country appears only where it changes the advice (pricing norms, regulations, platforms); otherwise drop it from the title.

## Fill-in
- Provider type: ...  Market: ...
- Primary query: "how to choose a <provider> in <market>"  Related: "questions to ask a <provider>", "<provider> cost in <market>", "red flags <provider>"
- URL: /guides/<slug>  Title: ...  Meta description: ...  h1: ...

## Skeleton
1. **Short answer:** the 5 things that matter most, in one paragraph (quotable).
2. **Criteria, one section each:** what it is, why it matters, how to verify it (ask for X, check Y), what a bad answer sounds like.
3. **Local specifics** that are true: typical price ranges in local currency with dates, contract norms, legal/tax points, where to verify a company (registry, directories).
4. **Questions to ask** (a list the reader can copy).
5. **Red flags** (first-hand).
6. **Where to find candidates:** independent directories and review sites (link to them; yes, including the ones you are listed on).
7. **Disclosure:** who wrote this and that you are a provider; one restrained CTA.

## Technical
Article JSON-LD with real author; BreadcrumbList; link from the service page; update when prices or rules change (substantively) `[G]`.

## Do not
- Turn it into a ranked list with yourself at the top `[WARNED]`.
- Clone it per city with the city name swapped `[G]`.
- Use "best" or "#1" about yourself anywhere on the page.
