# Reviews kit - compliant requests for Google, B2B directories, Trustpilot

Last verified 2026-10-20. The rules are platform policy, not taste:
- `[G]` Google prohibits incentives ("payment, discounts, free goods and/or services"), gating ("Discourage or prohibit negative reviews, or selectively solicit positive reviews"), conflict-of-interest reviews (incl. "a contractual or consultory relationship" - staff, partners, agencies you work with) and staff quotas. Penalties: new reviews blocked, existing ones unpublished, a consumer warning. [Contribution policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114) [Restrictions](https://support.google.com/business/answer/14114287?hl=en)
- `[OFFICIAL]` Trustpilot: invite only people "who have had a genuine experience"; invite "everyone in the same way"; no cherry-picking, no incentives, no family/staff/shareholders; penalties up to a Consumer Warning or termination. [Trustpilot guidelines v7, 2025-05](https://legal.trustpilot.com/for-reviewers/guidelines-for-businesses)
- `[OFFICIAL]` Clutch verifies reviewer identity; reviews weigh most in its score; Premier Verified needs >= 3 reviews with one in the last 12 months. [Clutch Help](https://help.clutch.co/en/knowledge/get-verified)
- `[WARNED]` Bursts get blocked: 2.75x baseline for 3 months -> 30-day posting block and 57.5% of 595 reviews removed (Sterling Sky 2026-09-28). `[DATA]` Review recency and steady velocity rank above total count ([Whitespark, 2025-11](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/); [Sterling Sky, 2025-11](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/what-gets-you-ranking-for-near-me-2025/)).
- `[DATA]` Text reviews beat star-only ([Sterling Sky, 2025-11](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/what-gets-you-ranking-for-near-me-2025/)); `[CLAIM]` keywords inside reviews did not help in a one-listing test ([Sterling Sky, 2023-01](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/does-the-text-of-a-google-review-affect-map-pack-rankings/)) (so never ask for them).

## Ground rules (print these)
1. Same ask, same words, to **every** customer after delivery - not only the happy ones.
2. No incentive of any kind, not even "enter a draw". No "if you're happy, review us; if not, call us" (that is gating).
3. Never suggest wording, stars, or keywords. Never write it for them. Never post from the office Wi-Fi on their behalf.
4. No staff, family, partners, vendors, or anyone with a "contractual or consultory relationship".
5. One ask + one reminder at most; then stop.
6. Steady cadence: spread requests over the delivery calendar; never batch a backlog into one week.
7. Reply to every review, including negative ones, factually and briefly `[DATA]` 89% of consumers expect replies ([BrightLocal, 2026-02](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)).
8. Keep a log (date asked, platform, outcome) so velocity stays natural and nobody is asked twice.

## Which platform to ask for `[INFERENCE from the citation data]`
- Everyone: Google (GBP) first - most-cited source in AI answers (28.6% of AI directory citations, BrightLocal 2026-08) and a map-pack factor.
- B2B services/agencies: Clutch (most cited for agency prompts), then GoodFirms.
- SaaS: G2 / Capterra.
- Consumer / e-commerce: Trustpilot or the marketplace's own system.
- Ask each customer for **one** platform (the one that fits them); rotating platforms across customers is fine, splitting one customer across three is annoying.

## Message templates

### WhatsApp - Google review (after delivery)
> Hi <Name>, thanks again for working with us on <project>. If you have two minutes, would you leave an honest review of your experience on Google? It helps other businesses decide whether we're a fit. Link: <GBP review link>. No pressure, and thank you either way - <Your name>

### Email - Google review
Subject: A quick favour, <Name>?

> Hi <Name>,
>
> Now that <project> is live, we'd value an honest review of how the engagement went - what worked and what didn't. Reviews are the main way other businesses check us out.
>
> Google: <GBP review link> (two minutes, no account beyond Gmail needed)
>
> Thank you either way, and we're here if anything needs attention.
>
> <Your name>, <Brand>

### Email - Clutch / GoodFirms (B2B directory)
Subject: Would you review <Brand> on Clutch?

> Hi <Name>,
>
> Clutch is where companies compare agencies, and it only lists reviews it has verified with the client directly. Could we send you a review invitation for <project>? It takes about 10 minutes (a short form, or a 15-minute call with a Clutch analyst), and it asks for your honest view of the whole engagement, including what we could have done better.
>
> If yes, reply and we'll have Clutch send the link to this address. If you'd rather not, no problem at all.
>
> <Your name>

### Email - Trustpilot (invite everyone the same way)
Subject: How did we do? Your honest review on Trustpilot

> Hi <Name>,
>
> We invite every client to share their experience on Trustpilot after a project ends. Your review is published as you write it, good or bad: <Trustpilot review link>
>
> Thank you for the time,
> <Your name>, <Brand>

### One reminder (7-10 days later, then stop)
> Hi <Name>, just a gentle nudge on the review request from last week - entirely optional. Thanks again for the project. - <Your name>

## Replying to reviews
- Positive: thank them, name one specific thing about the project, no keywords, no marketing.
- Negative: acknowledge, state the facts briefly, offer to continue offline, never argue or disclose private details. For a review that breaks policy (fake, off-topic, conflict of interest), flag it through the platform rather than replying angrily.

## Never
- Pay, discount, gift, or run a draw for reviews `[G]` `[OFFICIAL]`.
- Gate or pre-screen `[G]`.
- Ask staff, family, partners, suppliers `[G]`.
- Review swaps with other businesses `[G]` conflict of interest.
- Buy reviews `[WARNED]`; India's BIS IS 19000:2022 bars purchased reviews.
- Put review stars from your own reviews into your site's structured data `[G]` ineligible.
- Keyword-stuff replies or ask reviewers to mention services/cities `[CLAIM]` no effect in a one-listing test of review text ([Sterling Sky, 2023-01](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/does-the-text-of-a-google-review-affect-map-pack-rankings/)), `[G]` suspicious.
