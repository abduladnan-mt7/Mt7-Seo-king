# Outreach kit - earned lists, digital PR, journalist sources

Last verified 2026-10-20. Why this exists: `[DATA]` "best X" lists are 43.8% of ChatGPT-cited page types; for agency prompts, lists were 48-77% of citations across engines; `[PROVEN]` "expert-curated best-of lists" is Whitespark's #1 AI-visibility factor; `[DATA]` branded web mentions (0.66) and YouTube mentions (0.74) correlate with AI visibility far above backlinks (0.22-0.27). `[WARNED]` Publishing your own ranked list is risky; `[G]` buying placements without disclosure is link spam.

## Rules
1. Pitch with **specifics the editor can verify**: measured results, named clients (with permission), dates, screenshots. Vague "we are a leading agency" mails are deleted.
2. **Paid placement = `rel="sponsored"` + visible disclosure** on the host page `[G]`. If the publisher won't mark it, don't buy it. Never pay for "followed" links or list positions sold as editorial.
3. Ask for a **brand-name anchor** or a plain URL; never request keyword anchors `[WARNED]` exact-anchor links.
4. Accept **nofollow / mention-only** gladly - nofollow is a hint `[G]`, and mentions are what AI engines correlate with `[DATA]`.
5. Keep a log: date, outlet, contact, status, URL, link attribute.
6. Never seed Reddit/forums with disguised promotion `[WARNED]`; participate as yourself where you have real expertise, disclosed.
7. One real asset per quarter beats weekly pitches: original data, a free tool, a measured case `[DATA]` research-backed content earns more links.

## A. Inclusion in a third-party "best / top X in <city/country>" list

Find targets: search the exact query, note every list that is not a directory, check it was updated in the last 12 months, find the author.

Subject: <Your city> <category> list - a candidate with verifiable numbers

> Hi <Name>,
>
> Your "<list title>" (updated <month>) is the list people in <city> actually use, which is why I'm writing rather than publishing one of our own.
>
> <Brand> is a <one-line description, founded <year>, based in <city>>. Three things an editor can check:
> - <Client A>: <what was built>, <measured result with date and method>, <link to case study>.
> - <Client B>: <same>.
> - <Independent proof: reviews on Google/Clutch, a public GitHub repo, a talk, press>.
>
> If it fits your criteria, we'd be glad to be considered; happy to supply screenshots, a client reference, or answer questions. If your list has sponsored placements, please tell me how they're labelled - we only take editorial inclusion or clearly marked sponsored slots.
>
> Thanks for the work on the list,
> <Name, role, phone>

Follow-up once after 10 days; then move on.

## B. Digital PR / data story

Build one small, honest dataset you uniquely have (your own project metrics, a survey of your customers, a scrape of public data in your niche). Publish the method and the raw table on your site (the linkable asset), then pitch the finding, not the company.

Subject: Data: <one-line finding> (n=<sample>, <period>)

> Hi <Name>,
>
> We looked at <dataset> across <n> <units> between <dates> and found <finding with a number>. The full table and method are here: <URL>.
>
> Two angles that might suit <outlet>: <angle 1>; <angle 2>. I can share the spreadsheet and a chart in your house style, and I'm available for a quote today.
>
> <Name, role, phone>

Rules: never invent or extrapolate beyond the sample; date everything; expect most coverage to be a mention, not a link `[CLAIM]` thin before/after data for digital PR.

## C. Journalist-source platforms (Qwoted, Featured, Source of Sources, local equivalents)

`[CLAIM]` Vendor approval rates only; HARO's successor Connectively closed 2024-12-09. Treat as cheap, low-yield.

Template answer (reply within the hour; 80-150 words; first-hand only):

> <Direct answer to the question in one sentence.>
> <One concrete example from your own work with a number or date.>
> <One caveat or counter-intuitive point that shows experience.>
> - <Full name>, <role>, <Brand> (<city>), <URL>, <LinkedIn>

Decline questions outside your experience; invented expertise is both `[G]` deceptive and a waste of the editor's trust.

## D. Founder-led mentions you control

- LinkedIn long-form (500-2,000 words) under the founder's name: `[DATA]` Pulse long-form is 50-66% of cited LinkedIn content and individuals are cited more than company pages (Semrush 2026-03). One per month on something first-hand.
- YouTube: a demo or walkthrough per core service with the brand name in the title; `[DATA]` YouTube mentions ~0.737 correlation with AI visibility (Ahrefs 2025-12).
- Talks, podcasts, open-source READMEs, community directories where you genuinely belong - each is a mention with a verifiable byline.

## E. Client credit links (if you build things for clients)

- "Built by <Brand>" in a client footer: brand anchor, `rel="nofollow"` (or `sponsored` if it was part of the deal), never a keyword anchor, never site-wide on hundreds of pages `[G]` widely distributed footer links = link spam.
- Ask clients for a **case-study link** from a relevant page or a LinkedIn post instead - more value as a mention, no spam risk.

## F. Broken-link outreach (the best free tactic when you have a real replacement)

Supply is real: 66.5% of links to 2.06M sites have rotted since 2013 `[DATA]` (Ahrefs; `references/free-link-sources.md` [33]). Reply and conversion rates are vendor-reported only `[CLAIM]`.
1. Find dead targets in your topic: outbound links on competitors' and resource pages that return 404/410 (Ahrefs Webmaster Tools reports broken outgoing links on sites you verify; free crawlers on resource pages; "<topic> resources" / "useful links" searches) `[PROVEN]`.
2. See what the dead page offered (Wayback Machine). Only proceed if a page you already have, or will build anyway, genuinely replaces it.
3. List the pages that still link to it (Bing Webmaster Tools backlink lookup works for any URL; Ahrefs' free backlink checker shows the top few).
4. One email per linking page, no follow-up spam:

```
Subject: Broken link on <their page title>

Hi <name>,
Your <page> links to <dead URL> ("<anchor>"), which now returns a 404.
If a replacement helps: <your URL> covers <the same thing> - <one specific detail>.
Either way, thought you'd want to know.
<name>, <role>, <site>
```
5. Log it in the outreach sheet; never offer payment or a link back for the fix `[G]`.

## Never
- Buy links, list positions without disclosure, "guest post packages", link insertions, PBNs `[WARNED]` `[G]`.
- Publish a self-ranking "Top 10" `[WARNED]`.
- Seed forums / Reddit / Quora with sock puppets `[WARNED]`.
- Claim awards or "as seen in" logos without a linkable source `[INFERENCE]`.
