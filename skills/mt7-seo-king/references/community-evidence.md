# Community and practitioner evidence (tier 3 - "second until proved")

Last verified 2026-10-20. Tags: `[PROVEN]` = several independent practitioners report specifics and it does not contradict Google's docs; `[CLAIM]` = anecdotal or vendor-sourced, unproven; `[WARNED]` = widely reported to cause demotion, deindexing or suspension. Access caveat: Reddit, its mirrors and Indie Hackers were blocked during research; Reddit evidence is one mirror snapshot plus thread titles. The useful community layer is named practitioners showing data (Glenn Gabe, Lily Ray, Joy Hawkins/Sterling Sky, Darren Shaw/Whitespark, Cyrus Shepard/Zyppy, Marie Haynes), Local Search Forum and Hacker News. Several sources sell the matching service (flagged where so).

Where community and Google conflict, this skill follows Google (section 9).

## 1. New, low-authority site (first 6-12 months)

- `[PROVEN]` Start with long-tail questions with weak competition inside one tight topic. Vendor zero-link test: 50 interlinked posts -> ~1,500 visits/month and 400 ranking keywords by month 6, but the main term stuck on page 2-3 - links were needed to break the ceiling. [VH Info, 2025-12](https://vh-info.com/2025/12/14/zero-backlink-experiment/) (vendor) HN founders credit low competition. [HN, 2025-05](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43970837) Zyppy (400+ sites): tight topical focus correlated with winning, r = 0.25. [Zyppy, 2026-04](https://signal.zyppy.com/p/winning-google) Survivorship bias likely.
- `[CLAIM]` Claim brand results early: an HN case of a clone outranking a 2-week-old official site; advice = verify Search Console, official profiles + Organization/sameAs, ask linkers to point at the real domain. [HN, 2026-03](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47232158) No confirmed fix.
- `[CLAIM]` HARO replacements (Qwoted, Featured, Source of Sources); Connectively closed 2024-12-09; only vendor approval/link-rate data. [blckalpaca, 2026](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/seo-geo/off-page-seo-link-building/haro-alternatives-2026-featured-qwoted-and-source-of-sources)
- `[CLAIM]` Data-led digital PR is widely used [BuzzStream, 2025](https://www.buzzstream.com/blog/state-of-digital-pr-2025/); no new-site before/after.
- `[WARNED]` Copying competitors' "foundational"/profile/forum links onto a brand-new site. [r/SEO snapshot, 2026-01](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1qpuwun/snapshots/2026-01-29T21%3A31%3A39.34031Z)

## 2. Service business / agency

- `[PROVEN]` One dedicated page per service that answers buyer questions plainly - #2 AI-visibility factor (Whitespark 2026, 47 experts) [Whitespark, 2025-11-06](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/); for 31 "best agencies for X" prompts, 57% of ChatGPT citations were the agency's own site when that page answered the query [Onely, 2026-10-16](https://www.onely.com/blog/how-chatgpt-decides-which-brands-to-recommend/) (vendor); Google's predefined GBP services help rankings. [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/blog/increase-local-seo-rankings-with-predefined-services/)
- `[PROVEN]` (correlational) Placement on others' "best X" lists: for agency prompts, lists were 70% of Perplexity citations, 77% Gemini, 59% AI Mode, 48% AIO (Onely); "expert-curated best-of lists" = Whitespark's #1 AI factor.
- `[WARNED]` (contested) Your own "best X" list ranking yourself #1: Lily Ray tied 29-49% SaaS visibility drops (Jan-Feb 2026) to self-ranking listicles; Gabe: such sites "got crushed" in Jan 2026 and had not recovered by May [GSQi, 2026-06-10](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/core-roars-back-google-may-2026-core-update-analysis/); AIO cited them 38% less Jun-Sep 2026 and omitted the publisher 83% of the time [PPC Land, 2026-10](https://ppc.land/google-ai-overviews-cut-self-ranking-listicle-citations-38-lily-ray-finds/); ChatGPT listicle citations -30% Dec -> Jan [Seer, 2026-02-25](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/the-listicle-window-is-closing-in-ai-search-30-decline-mom). Counter: listicles were < 10% of affected sites' posts [Grow & Convert, 2026-05](https://www.growandconvert.com/seo/self-promotional-listicles/); Indig: 62% of vendor lists gained traffic. Verdict: don't crown yourself; an honest comparison that names peers is a different page.
- `[CLAIM]` Case-study/portfolio pages bring leads (vendor case studies only); Zyppy: "offers a product or service" r = 0.39 and "proprietary assets" r = 0.36 with winning.
- `[CLAIM]` (mostly negative) Paying for Clutch: ~$10k spent on 12-month lock-ins for spam/no leads; Clutch says ROI ~12 months. [Trustpilot reviews of Clutch](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/clutch.co) (self-selected). The free profile is what matters: a third-party review source ("authority of third-party review sites" = Whitespark AI factor #5).

## 3. Local SEO (GBP)

- `[PROVEN]` basics (Whitespark 2026 pack/Maps): primary category #1, proximity #2, real address in the searched city #4, additional categories #8, review count with text #9, review recency #11, steady flow not bursts #14.
- Myth: Google Posts and owner-seeded Q&A as ranking levers - post frequency #148, seeded Q&A #142; the Q&A API was switched off 2025-11-03 and replaced by Gemini "Ask Maps". [The HOTH, 2025](https://www.thehoth.com/blog/google-cuts-qa-sections/)
- `[CLAIM]` (conflicting) Show vs hide address: Sterling Sky - hiding cut rankings/calls, restoring brought them back (2 listings) [Sterling Sky, 2026-09](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/hiding-address-google-business-ranking/); Whitespark has a counter-case; forum theory: a hidden address widens the radius. [LSF](https://localsearchforum.com/threads/do-service-areas-impact-local-rankings-darren-says-yes-joy-says-no.59764/) Follow Google's policy for your business type.
- `[WARNED]` Review bursts, in-store QR codes, staff-driven pushes, gating: 2.75x baseline for 3 months -> 30-day posting block, 57.5% of 595 reviews removed. [Sterling Sky, 2026-09-28](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/review-posting-blocks-are-surging-heres-the-data-plus-the-patterns-behind-them/)
- `[WARNED]` Suspension triggers: profile edits (even the phone), tracking strings on the website URL, accounts managing many profiles, prior suspensions; appeals ~5 days -> ~5 weeks; Product Expert advice: no edits during suspension waves [LSF, 2025-09/10](https://localsearchforum.com/threads/ive-been-noticing-a-huge-spike-in-suspensions-recently.62779/) [SEJ, 2025-03-21](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-business-profile-suspensions-rise-appeals-are-delayed/542602/); keyword-stuffed names.

## 4. Programmatic and city pages

- `[WARNED]` Templated city/variant pages and scaled AI content: Gabe's Aug 2026 spam-update cases lost 14K-200K+ queries, and the loss carried into AIO, AI Mode and ChatGPT [GSQi, 2026-08-31](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/); 850K AI local-news URLs -> manual action, ChatGPT citations vanished [GSQi, 2026-04-24](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/when-mt-ai-crumbles-chatgpt-follows/); r/bigseo "60% of the pages got deindexed" after 5 months of pSEO (title only) [thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/bigseo/comments/1t0xwsy/ran_programmatic_seo_for_5_months_60_of_the_pages/); `[G]` fan-out variation pages may be scaled content abuse. [AI guide, 2026-05](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)
- `[PROVEN]` Programmatic pages survive only with unique data per page; pruning is the fix - pastmaps (HN); a site deindexed from ~30,000 to 5 pages rebuilt around a 583-URL indexable core with the rest `noindex,follow`. [DEV](https://dev.to/kavelaltd/how-we-recovered-from-a-30000-to-5-google-deindex-on-a-programmatic-seo-site-40io)

## 5. Link building

- `[WARNED]` Keyword-anchor paid links, guest-post networks, legacy SEO links: Sterling Sky audits found 2025 updates suppressed local business sites by > 90% organic via site-wide classifiers with no Search Console notice; the warning sign was rising referring domains while traffic fell; none recovered; the local pack was unaffected. [Sterling Sky, 2026-09-30](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/google-core-algorithm-updates/)
- `[WARNED]` Parasite SEO / site reputation abuse: the "first-party oversight" exemption was removed; manual actions on Forbes Advisor, CNN Underscored. [SEL, 2024-11](https://searchengineland.com/?p=448432)
- `[WARNED]` (policy) Expired-domain abuse. [Google, 2024-03](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2024/03/core-update-spam-policies)
- `[CLAIM]` Earned editorial links from real coverage - universally endorsed, thin before/after data.

## 6. AI-search visibility

- `[PROVEN]` (large correlational) Off-site brand mentions beat backlinks: YouTube mentions ~0.737, branded web mentions ~0.66, DR ~0.27 (75K brands) [Ahrefs, 2025-12](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations); Whitespark: 3 of the top 5 AI factors are citations.
- `[PROVEN]` Organic rankings carry into AI answers (Gabe); one case where ChatGPT citations tracked Bing rankings, not Google. [GSQi, 2026-08-03](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/surging-in-chatgpt-dead-in-google/) -> Bing matters.
- `[CLAIM]` (strong, self-testable) Serve content as HTML - ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity couldn't read client-side-JS-rendered content; Gemini and Copilot partly. [GSQi, 2026-10-19](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/can-ai-search-platforms-render-javascript/) Don't block OAI-SearchBot.
- Hype: llms.txt - no citation effect across 300K domains [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/llms-txt/); Google ignores it; harmless.
- `[WARNED]` Seeding Reddit/forums for LLMs: Reddit catches ~25K spam posts/day; seeded posts get removed and called out. [Waxy, 2026-06](https://waxy.org/2026/06/companies-are-using-reddit-to-manipulate-chatgpt-and-google-ai-search/) [Ray, 2026-10-04](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/prediction-the-next-massive-google)
- Volatility: Reddit's ChatGPT citation share fell ~86% in Aug 2026; trackers disagree ~4x on baselines. [TechRepublic, 2026-08](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-chatgpt-reddit-citations-ai-search/)

## 7. Recovery after core / helpful-content updates

- `[PROVEN]` Recovery is rare, partial, and arrives at a later core update: 47 of Gabe's 380+ HCU-hit sites surged in Aug 2024, none fully [SEL, 2024-08](https://searchengineland.com/?p=445290); a larger wave in June 2025, +20-80% for some. [PPC Land, 2025-07](https://ppc.land/google-june-2025-core-update-brings-unexpected-recoveries-after-16-day-rollout/)
- `[CLAIM]` What recovered sites changed (Marie Haynes, 4 sites after Dec 2025): better customer service / business trust; own photos and video, first-hand testing; noindexed YMYL pages lacking expertise; simpler navigation/checkout, fuller About page; content that earned links. [SEJ, 2026-02-23](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/4-sites-that-recovered-from-googles-december-2025-core-update/567960/) Causality unproven; Zyppy found first-hand signals did NOT correlate with winning.
- Disavow: Google says it is only for manual actions; Sterling Sky reports algorithmic suppression from legacy links - unresolved. Use disavow only for a manual action or a clearly toxic paid-link history you can document.

## 8. Ranked lists

Top 10 community-proven tactics (by evidence strength):
1. Correct primary + secondary GBP categories, real verifiable address.
2. Steady natural flow of Google reviews with text, never bursts.
3. One page per service answering buyer questions + Google's predefined GBP services.
4. Earned placement on independent "best X" lists and industry sites.
5. Off-site brand mentions (web + YouTube).
6. New site: long-tail intents inside one tight topic.
7. Proprietary assets: original data, real case numbers, own media.
8. Programmatic pages only with unique data per page; prune/noindex the rest.
9. Rank in Google AND Bing (AI answers inherit both).
10. Recovery = business-quality fix + wait for the next core update.

Top 10 warned-against:
1. Scaled AI/templated pages incl. fan-out variation pages.
2. Self-ranking "best X" listicles.
3. Paid/exact-anchor links, PBNs, link insertions, guest-post farms.
4. Parasite SEO / site reputation abuse.
5. Keyword-stuffed GBP names.
6. Review bursts, gating, incentives, QR drives.
7. Fake/AI authors and credentials.
8. Seeding Reddit/forums for LLMs.
9. GBP risks: virtual offices, duplicates, frequent edits, tracking strings on the website URL.
10. Expired-domain abuse; "foundational"/forum links on a new site.

## 9. Community vs Google conflicts - follow Google

| Topic | Community says | Google says | Skill rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keywords in GBP name | #3 ranking factor | Violates guidelines, suspension | Exact name only |
| Hiding the address | Costs rankings (Sterling Sky) | SABs must hide it | Follow policy |
| llms.txt, "AEO/GEO tactics" | Vendors push them | Still SEO; llms.txt ignored | Hygiene only; data agrees with Google |
| Long-tail at scale | More pages, more rankings | Variant pages = scaled abuse | One page per distinct intent |
| Brand mentions | Mentions drive AI answers | Inauthentic mentions don't help | Earn them; never seed |
| Disavow | Algorithmic suppression from legacy links | Only for manual actions | Document before disavowing |

Most AI-visibility evidence is vendor-produced and correlational: it gives direction, not proof.
